Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy covers MobileStop Social Publisher ("the app"), an internal tool operated by MobileStop, Colombo, Sri Lanka.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by MobileStop staff to publish content to social media accounts owned by MobileStop. It is not offered to the public.

What data we access

  • Basic profile information of the MobileStop account that authorises the app (account ID, display name, avatar), used to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by the platform, used only to publish MobileStop's own posts and check their publishing status.
  • The photos, videos and captions MobileStop chooses to publish.

What we do not do

We do not collect data about other users, followers or viewers. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use any data for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on MobileStop's own private server and are never published. Media files are deleted from the server after they are published.

Removing access

MobileStop can revoke the app's access at any time from the platform's account settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). On revocation the stored tokens are deleted.
