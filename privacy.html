Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy covers two internal tools operated by MobileStop (Pvt) Ltd, Colombo, Sri Lanka (together, "the apps"):

  • MobileStop Social Publisher, which publishes MobileStop's own posts to MobileStop's own social media accounts.
  • MobileStop Ads Manager, which manages MobileStop's own TikTok advertising account through the TikTok API for Business.

Who uses the app

The apps are used only by authorised MobileStop staff, and only for accounts owned by MobileStop. They are not offered to the public or to any other business or advertiser.

What data the Social Publisher accesses

  • Basic profile information of the MobileStop account that authorises the app (account ID, display name, avatar), used to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by the platform, used only to publish MobileStop's own posts and check their publishing status.
  • The photos, videos and captions MobileStop chooses to publish.

What data the Ads Manager accesses

The Ads Manager connects only to MobileStop's own TikTok advertising account. It accesses:

  • Our ad account details: account ID, name, currency, timezone and balance, used to keep spending under a fixed daily limit.
  • Our own campaigns, ad groups and ads, and their status and budgets, so we can create and manage them. New ads are created paused and go live only after the owner approves them.
  • The product videos and images MobileStop uploads for use in its own ads.
  • Aggregated performance reports for our own ads (spend, impressions, clicks, click-through rate, cost per click and conversions), used for an internal daily summary.

Reports are aggregated totals. The Ads Manager does not access personal data about the people who see or click our ads, does not upload customer lists or build custom audiences, and does not access any other advertiser's account.

What we do not do

We do not collect personal data about other users, followers or viewers. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Data obtained through the apps is used only to run MobileStop's own accounts and our own advertising, and is never used to target or profile individuals.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on MobileStop's own private server and are never published or shared. Media files are deleted from the server after they are published. Ad performance reports are kept on the same private server for internal use only.

Removing access

MobileStop can revoke the apps' access at any time from the platform's account settings (for TikTok posting: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; for TikTok ads: TikTok Ads Manager or TikTok Business Center authorisation settings). On revocation the stored tokens are deleted.
